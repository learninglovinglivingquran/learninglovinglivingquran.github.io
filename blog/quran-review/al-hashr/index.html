Surah Al-Hashr

سُورَةُ الْحَشْر · Surah 59 · Madani · 24 ayat

The surah takes its name from the word الْحَشْر (al-ḥashr), which appears in the second ayah. The word is often translated as "the gathering." Its closing ayat (22–24) are well known for the many names of Allah they contain.

Each ayah review below looks at the Arabic of a single ayah: the words, their roots, and how they fit together. Start at the first ayah and read forward, or jump to the one you want.
