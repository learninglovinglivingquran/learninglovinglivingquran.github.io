Surah Ash-Shams

سُورَةُ الشَّمْس · Surah 91 · Makki · 15 ayat

A series of personal reflections on the ayat of this surah. Each post takes one ayah, or a few together, and sits with what it makes us think about. These are personal understanding and not tafsir.

    Ayah 7–8: Where does the soul get its sense of right and wrong?

    We don't need God to tell us right from wrong, the argument goes, because we already know. The ayah agrees that we know, and asks where that knowledge came from.
