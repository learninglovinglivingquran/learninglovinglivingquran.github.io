About

A pathway to the Quran: learn it, love it, live it.

The idea

LQ stands for learn, love, live the Quran.

The idea behind this site is that all of us, as Muslims and as human beings, need a pathway to the Quran. At a high level, that pathway has three steps:

  1. Learn. It starts with learning about the Quran.
  2. Love. Learning leads to loving it.
  3. Live. And loving it leads to living the Quran.

Each step grows out of the one before it. The first post on the blog goes into the pathway in more detail.

What you'll find here

Four kinds of posts, all written to serve that pathway:

  • Personal reflections: thoughts on the Quran and on life, not limited to Arabic or the Quran alone.
  • Arabic grammar and language: the building blocks of the language, explained with examples from the Quran.
  • Quran Review: slow, in-depth readings of the Quran, surah by surah and ayah by ayah, looking at the Arabic beneath them.
  • Quran in prayer: companion pages for a weekly study circle, going through the surahs we recite in prayer word by word, so the meaning can come with us the next time we pray.

Who it's for

Anyone. If you're new to Islam, curious about the Quran, just starting Arabic, or looking to go deeper, there's a place on the pathway for you.

About the author

I'm Shaheer. I'm a student of the Quran and Arabic, sharing what I learn along the way. I'm not a scholar, and I'd rather you check what I write than take it on trust.
